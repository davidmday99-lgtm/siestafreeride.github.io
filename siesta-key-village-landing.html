The heart of the Key

One strip.
All the energy.

Siesta Key Village is the island’s walkable center for eating, shopping and nightlife—close enough to explore without ever getting back in the car.

Start with coffee or the farmers market, browse local shops in the afternoon, then settle in for seafood, frozen drinks and live music after sunset.

Sunday farmers market.

Fresh produce, baked goods, local honey, handmade crafts and live music bring the Village to life every Sunday morning.

  • Arrive early for easier browsing
  • Bring cash for smaller vendors
  • Stay for breakfast or lunch nearby
  • Call us when you are ready to head home
