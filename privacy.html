Your information

Privacy policy.

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Information we collect

Siesta Free Ride may receive information you choose to provide when you call, text, email or submit a website form. Our website also uses analytics and advertising technologies that may receive information such as the page visited, approximate location derived from an IP address, browser and device type, referring page and interactions with the website.

How we use information

We use information to answer ride requests, provide transportation services, improve the website, understand which content is useful, measure advertising and show relevant Siesta Free Ride advertising.

Analytics and advertising tools

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand website use. With your advertising-tracking consent, we also use the Meta Pixel to measure visits and build audiences for advertising on Facebook and Instagram. Meta may process information according to its own privacy policy and advertising settings.

Your choices

You may accept or decline Meta Pixel advertising tracking through the website’s privacy choices notice. You can reopen that notice using the Privacy choices control in the footer. We do not load the Meta Pixel when a browser signals Global Privacy Control. You can also limit cookies and similar technologies through your browser and Meta account settings.

Data sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, communications, analytics and advertising. We do not sell personal contact information provided for ride requests. We do not intentionally send health, financial, consumer-report or other sensitive information through the Meta Pixel.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email siestafreeride@gmail.com or call 941-600-8380.
