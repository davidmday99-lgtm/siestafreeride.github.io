01 / Beaches + water

Before your
toes hit sand.

Access, parking, rules, water conditions and the questions that come up before a beach day.

01Is Siesta Key Beach free?
Yes. Siesta Beach has free public access, no entrance fee, no beach pass and free main-lot parking. The lot fills quickly from January–April, so arrive before 9am or call us for a ride.
02What is the best beach on Siesta Key?
Siesta Beach is repeatedly ranked among the best beaches in the United States. Its 99% pure quartz sand stays cool even in hot weather. For a quieter experience, try Crescent Beach or Turtle Beach.
03What time does Siesta Beach close?
The beach is open from 8am until sunset. Lifeguards are normally on duty from 10am–5pm, and the parking lot gates close at midnight.
04Is Siesta Key dog-friendly?
Dogs are not allowed on Sarasota County public beaches, including Siesta and Turtle Beach, except service animals. For off-leash Gulf access, visit Brohard Paw Park in Venice.
05What is the water temperature?
Gulf water ranges from about 65°F in January to 86°F in August. Most visitors find it comfortable for swimming from April through November; spring and fall average roughly 75–80°F.
06Is there parking at Siesta Beach?
Yes. The free main lot has about 800 spaces, but from January–April it can fill by 9–10am. Overflow parking is limited, so Siesta Free Ride is the easiest way to skip the lot entirely.
07Can I drink alcohol on the beach?
Yes. Alcohol is permitted on Siesta Key Beach. Please drink responsibly and use plastic or aluminum containers—glass is not allowed.
08Are there jellyfish at Siesta Key?
Jellyfish can appear year-round and are most common in August–September. Most are harmless moon jellies. Avoid any blue or purple Portuguese man-o’-war, and watch for purple warning flags.

09What’s the best way to get around?
Call Siesta Free Ride at 941-600-8380 for free, air-conditioned rides anywhere on the Key—beaches, Village restaurants, bars and hotels. Tips are appreciated. Bikes and electric scooters are also available in the Village.
10What are Siesta Free Ride’s hours?
Season, Oct 30–Apr 15: 9am–1am daily. Off-season, Apr 16–Oct 29: 10am–1am daily. Start and stop times may vary, and airport rides are by appointment.
11Where is Siesta Key located?
Siesta Key is a barrier island off Sarasota on Florida’s Gulf Coast. Two bridges connect it to the mainland: the north bridge from US-41 and the south bridge at Stickney Point Road.
12How far is Siesta Key from Tampa?
About 75 miles south of Tampa—roughly 1 hour 15 minutes via I-75. Sarasota-Bradenton International Airport is much closer, about 20 minutes away.
13How far is Siesta Key from Sarasota?
Siesta Key is about 8 miles from downtown Sarasota, usually a 20-minute drive. The north bridge connects to US-41; Stickney Point connects toward Clark Road and I-75.

03 / Food + visiting

Make the most
of the Key.

Where to eat, when to visit and the traditions, safety and weather that shape an island trip.

14Where should I eat on Siesta Key?
Top picks include Siesta Key Oyster Bar for oysters and music, Old Salty Dog for waterfront dining, Daiquiri Deck for frozen drinks, Gilligan’s for tropical vibes and Sun Garden Cafe for breakfast.
15What is the Siesta Key Drum Circle?
Every Sunday at sunset, hundreds gather on Siesta Beach with drums, instruments and dancing. It is free to attend, and everyone is welcome to join. Arrive early for a good spot.
16Is Siesta Key safe?
Siesta Key is generally a safe, family-friendly destination. Use normal travel precautions: lock your car, keep valuables attended, use sunscreen and swim near lifeguards when possible.
17When is the best time to visit?
January–April offers the best weather and biggest crowds. May–September is hotter, quieter and often more affordable. October–December is the local sweet spot: warm, uncrowded and comfortable.
