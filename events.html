A year on the Key

Plan by
the season.

Jan—Mar

Peak season

Art festivals, boat shows, the Sarasota Film Festival and a Village buzzing every night. Book dinner early.

Apr—Jun

Spring into summer

Spring break winds down, turtle nesting begins in May and Memorial Day launches the summer season.

Jul—Sep

Local summer

Warm Gulf water, afternoon storms, quieter crowds and spectacular July 4 fireworks over Sarasota Bay.

October

Perfect weather

Shoulder season begins, Halloween arrives in the Village and restaurants roll out seasonal specials.

November

Crystal Classic

World-class sculptors turn Siesta’s quartz sand into massive works of art. Tens of thousands attend.

December

Holiday season

Village decorations, special dinner menus and New Year’s Eve celebrations welcome the returning snowbirds.
