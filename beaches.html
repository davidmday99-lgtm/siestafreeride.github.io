Four stretches of shoreline

Pick your
perfect sand.

01

#1 in the USA

Siesta
Beach

Famous for 99% pure quartz crystal sand—fine, white, squeaky underfoot and cool even on the hottest days.

  • Lifeguards 10am–5pm
  • 800+ free parking spaces
  • Concessions + restrooms
  • Volleyball + playground
  • Open 8am to sunset
Parking fills by 9am in peak season.
02

Scenic + secluded

Turtle
Beach

The south-end local favorite: more secluded, less crowded and excellent for shelling and fossil hunting.

  • Boat ramp access
  • Campground nearby
  • Picnic tables + grills
  • Coarser natural sand
  • Easier parking
Dogs are not permitted, except service animals.
03

Family favorite

Crescent
Beach

A wide, less-crowded shoreline between Siesta and Turtle Beach, with Point of Rocks at its southern end.

  • Excellent snorkeling
  • Wide open shoreline
  • Beautiful sunset views
  • More space for families
  • Limited restrooms
No lifeguards—swim at your own risk.
04

Wild + natural

Midnight
Pass

The undeveloped southern tip of Siesta Key: quiet, natural and reached by walking south from Turtle Beach.

  • Excellent birdwatching
  • Kayak-friendly water
  • No crowds
  • No facilities
  • No lifeguards
Bring everything you need and pack it back out.

The local parking rule

Before
9am.

That is the simplest way to get a main-lot space during January–April.

01

Use the free main lot

About 800 spaces on Beach Road, first-come and first-served. There are no reservations.

02

Know the overflow

Beach Road and Village side streets can add a 10–15 minute walk to the sand.

03

Try Turtle Beach

The lot is smaller, but demand is typically much lower than Siesta Beach.

04

Skip parking entirely

Call Siesta Free Ride for curbside beach drop-off and pickup.
