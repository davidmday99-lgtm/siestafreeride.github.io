Monday Beach & Water Brief

Start the week with an early beach window, warm Gulf water, and a flexible plan for passing storms.

Weather report

Daytimearound 89°F
Overnightnear 80°F

Warm, humid conditions favored scattered showers and thunderstorms. Summer heat and humidity can push the feels-like temperature higher, so bring water, shade, and sun protection.

Lightning rule: If you hear thunder, leave the beach and water and move into a substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Water report

Windwest to southwest near 8–13 knots
Nearshore seasabout 2 feet

Gulf water is seasonably very warm in early August. Waves, currents, water quality, and flags can differ by beach and hour—check the posted lifeguard flag before swimming.

Boaters and paddlers: Winds and seas may be higher in and near thunderstorms.

Plan the day

Favor an earlier beach window, keep the afternoon flexible, and save an indoor or Village stop for any passing storm. Never shelter under a beach umbrella during lightning.
