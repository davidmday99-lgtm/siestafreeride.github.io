Plan around the heat

Beach early. Cool down midday.

The morning high tide arrived around 10:19 a.m.; the lowest tide followed near 6:26 p.m. Use those times to plan shoreline walks, but always follow posted beach flags and lifeguard guidance. Reapply sunscreen after swimming and keep cold water close.
