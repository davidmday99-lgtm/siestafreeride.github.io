A better beach day

Go early, cool down often

Summer sun feels strongest from late morning through the afternoon. Bring plenty of water, use broad-spectrum sunscreen, and take regular shade breaks. Warm Gulf water may feel inviting, but it does not replace hydration or a break from the heat.
