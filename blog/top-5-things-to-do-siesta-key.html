Top 5 Things to Do in Siesta Key This Weekend

Updated September 15, 2026 • 5 min read

Siesta Key isn't just another Florida beach town—it's a weekend paradise waiting to be explored. Whether you're visiting for the first time or you're a seasoned local looking for fresh ideas, here are our top 5 picks for an unforgettable weekend.

1. Sunset Beach Stroll at Siesta Beach

There is a reason Siesta Beach consistently ranks among America’s best beaches. The quartz-crystal sand stays cool underfoot, and the sunset views over the Gulf are unforgettable. Grab a blanket, arrive about 45 minutes before sunset and review our Siesta Key public beach access and parking guide. Parking fills quickly on weekends, so our free local ride service can handle the drop-off and pickup.

2. Explore Siesta Key Village

The Village is the heart of Siesta Key’s dining and nightlife scene. Start with coffee, browse local boutiques, then choose from patios, seafood restaurants and live-music venues. Everything is walkable once you arrive; use our Siesta Key Village guide to plan the day.

3. Kayak Through the Mangroves

For a change of pace from the beach, head to nearby Point of Rocks or Turtle Beach for guided kayak tours through serene mangrove tunnels. You'll spot herons, ospreys, and if you're lucky, manatees. Several local outfitters offer rentals and guided tours—most include all equipment and a brief safety lesson. It's a perfect 2-3 hour adventure that feels worlds away from the mainland.

4. Dinner and Live Music in the Village

Turn dinner into an island night by choosing a Village patio or live-music venue. Restaurant schedules and performers change, so check the venue’s current listing before heading out. Our Siesta Key events guide covers the weekly rhythm and major seasonal events.

5. Sunday Morning Farmers Market

The Siesta Key Farmers Market brings local food, handmade goods and music to Davidson Plaza on Sunday mornings. Hours can change, so verify the market’s current listing before you go. It is an easy final stop before brunch or the beach.
